Common questions

The paywall does not show prices.

Check your connection and use the paywall’s retry action. Missing prices are not replaced with estimates. Existing rights and saved files are handled separately. Tell us the status you see and your app version.

I cannot see word highlighting.

Manual colour emphasis differs from highlighting the word currently being spoken. Active word highlighting needs valid word timings and a suitable setting in the selected look. Editing the text can invalidate existing timings. Check the look and the status shown in the app.

I cannot save an export.

Check free storage and access to Photos or your selected file provider. If your export is still available in the app, try saving or sharing it again first. Export files are kept temporarily; save important results outside the app.
