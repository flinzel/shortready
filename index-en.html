Good to know

Clear answers.

Does recognition work on every device?

Speech recognition depends on your device, operating system and selected language. Review the recognised text and timing before exporting.

Can I correct captions myself?

Yes. In the editor, you can correct words and timing, split or move captions, and undo changes.

What happens to a file I already exported?

A new export does not overwrite your existing file. While that file is still available in the app, you can watch, save or share it again. Export files are kept there temporarily. Save important results to Photos or Files.

Where can I get help with my subscription?

The support page explains restore and subscription management. You can also contact us about a technical issue without an account or purchase.
