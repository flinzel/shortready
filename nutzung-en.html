Licence & subscriptions

Licence terms

The Apple Standard End User Licence Agreement (EULA) applies to use of the app unless a different valid licence is specified in the App Store. Your statutory rights are unaffected.

Free and Pro

Free lets you preview all 20 looks. Creator Bold and Clean Short are available for export: one successful export per local calendar day, up to 30 seconds of edited output, and up to 1080p with a watermark.

Pro includes unlocked Pro features such as watermark-free output, all looks and caption files. 4K requires a suitable source and a device with the necessary technical capabilities.

Pricing and billing

Pro is available as a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription. All three periods provide the same Pro features. There is no free trial. The full price in the Store currency and the period shown in the app and Apple’s purchase dialog before purchase apply. A yearly subscription is billed annually in advance. Any equivalent monthly comparison does not mean monthly instalments.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless you turn off automatic renewal in time. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Cancellation normally stops the next renewal; refunds and statutory rights depend on the rules applicable to your purchase.

Apple: Cancel a subscription · Report a problem with a purchase

Restoring purchases and keeping files

Use Restore Purchases in the app if existing access is missing. A new purchase decision does not change a file already successfully created. However, export files are only available temporarily in the app. Save results you want to keep to Photos, Files or another location you choose.

Your statutory rights

This information page does not exclude mandatory consumer, warranty, withdrawal or other rights. The App Store and your purchase process identify the contractual information and the provider applicable to your purchase. For an app problem, contact ShortReady support .
